
# Customer Complaint Priority Assessment

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the priority order for addressing the seven customer complaints received by BrightPath Digital Solutions.

Priorities are based on potential financial risk, customer impact, service disruption and reputational risk.

## 2. Management Priority Ranking

| Priority | Complaint ID | Issue | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C001 | Delayed subscription activation | Customer has paid but cannot access the service. |
| 2 | C003 | Duplicate subscription charges | Potential direct financial loss to customers. |
| 3 | C005 | Delayed refund processing | Outstanding financial requests and poor communication. |
| 4 | C006 | Account access difficulties | Customer cannot access the service. |
| 5 | C002 | Unanswered enquiries | Repeated unanswered contact damages customer trust. |
| 6 | C004 | Inconsistent agent information | Indicates a knowledge and communication gap. |
| 7 | C007 | Missing expiry notifications | Requires preventive operational improvements. |

## 3. Immediate Management Actions

The following issues require urgent investigation:

- C001: Investigate payment confirmation and subscription activation.
- C003: Verify duplicate transactions and resolve confirmed duplicate charges.
- C005: Review outstanding refund requests and establish a tracking process.

## 4. System Improvement Recommendations

- Introduce transaction verification and duplicate-payment safeguards.
- Establish automated payment and activation monitoring.
- Introduce a refund tracking and escalation process.
- Implement account recovery procedures.
- Establish customer enquiry response targets.
- Create standardised support documentation.
- Introduce automated subscription expiry reminders.

## 5. Conclusion

The priority assessment provides management with a structured approach to addressing customer complaints.

The ranking should be reviewed as additional information becomes available, particularly the number of affected customers, financial exposure and severity of service disruption.
